# Script: Which penguin is the heavy one?

## Opening

[S01] [puzzle+goal] Three penguin species were weighed in the Palmer Archipelago, off Antarctica. One of them outweighs the others by more than a kilogram. Which one, and how sure can we be? {C05} {C06}
[S01] [roadmap] First, weight by species. Then, the Gentoo gap. {break}

## T1 · Weight by species

[S02] [announce+introduce] Weight by species. Researchers at Palmer Station weighed three hundred and forty two penguins between two thousand seven and two thousand nine. {C06}
[S02] [predict] Before the dots land, take a guess. Adelie, Chinstrap, or Gentoo: which is the heavy one? {look:3}
[S03] [reveal] Each dot is one bird. Adelie and Chinstrap penguins land together, both close to three thousand seven hundred grams. {C01} {C02} {beat}
[S03] [explain] Gentoos land far above them, at about five thousand grams. {C03} {beat}
[S04] [wrap-up+bridge] So it's the Gentoo. Next: how big is that gap, really? {look:2} {break}

## T2 · The Gentoo gap

[S05] [announce+introduce] The Gentoo gap. A difference only means something once you know how precisely it was measured.
[S05] [explain] On average, Gentoos are about one thousand three hundred and seventy five grams heavier than Adelies. {C05} {beat}
[S06] [explain] The band shows where the true difference plausibly sits: from about twelve hundred and sixty to about fourteen hundred and ninety grams. It doesn't come anywhere near zero. {C05} {look:3}
[S06] [wrap-up+bridge] Even at the low end, that's more than a kilogram. {break}

## Conclusion

[S07] [synthesis] Adelie and Chinstrap penguins weigh about the same. {C01} {C02}
[S07] [reveal] The heavy one is the Gentoo, by more than a kilogram, and the data leave little room for doubt. {C05}
