# Script: Which penguin is the heavy one?

## Opening

[S01] [goal] Three penguin species were weighed in the Palmer Archipelago, off Antarctica. Which one is the heavy one, and how sure can we be? {C06}
[S01] [roadmap] First, weight by species. Then, the Gentoo gap. {break}

## T1 · Weight by species

[S02] [announce+introduce] Weight by species. Researchers at Palmer Station weighed three hundred and forty two penguins between two thousand seven and two thousand nine. {C06}
[S03] [explain] Each dot is one bird. Adelie and Chinstrap penguins sit together, both close to three thousand seven hundred grams. {C01} {C02} {beat}
[S03] [explain] Gentoo penguins sit far above them, at about five thousand grams. {C03} {beat}
[S04] [wrap-up+bridge] So the Gentoo is the heavy one. Next: how big is that gap? {look:2} {break}

## T2 · The Gentoo gap

[S05] [announce+introduce] The Gentoo gap. To trust a difference, we need to know how precisely it was measured.
[S05] [explain] On average, Gentoos are about one thousand three hundred and seventy five grams heavier than Adelies. {C05} {beat}
[S06] [explain] The band shows where the true difference plausibly sits: from about twelve hundred and sixty to about fourteen hundred and ninety grams, nowhere near zero. {C05} {look:3}
[S06] [wrap-up+bridge] Even at the low end, that is more than a kilogram. {break}

## Conclusion

[S07] [synthesis] Adelie and Chinstrap penguins weigh about the same. The Gentoo is the heavy species, by more than a kilogram. {C01} {C02} {C05}
